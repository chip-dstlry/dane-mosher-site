At fifteen, Dane Mosher read Life Extension by Durk Pearson and Sandy Shaw and came away with a conviction he has never let go: health is not a mystery to be endured. It's a system that can be measured, understood, and engineered.

That conviction carried through a Family Medicine residency at East Tennessee State University, certification with the Institute for Functional Medicine, and board service with the American Academy of Environmental Medicine. Since 2011 he has served the autism community through The Johnson Center in Austin, Texas — where careful measurement and root-cause repair still had answers after conventional approaches ran out.
