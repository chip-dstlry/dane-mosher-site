Biographical notes

A conviction, held since age fifteen.

  • Age 15Life Extension — Durk Pearson & Sandy Shaw
  • ResidencyEast Tennessee State University, Family Medicine
  • 2011The Johnson Center, Austin, TX
  • 2015IFM certification, functional medicine
  • 2017—Board, American Academy of Environmental Medicine

At fifteen, Dane Mosher read Life Extension by Durk Pearson and Sandy Shaw and came away with a conviction he has never let go: health is not a mystery to be endured but a system that can be measured, understood, and engineered.

That conviction carried through Family Medicine residency at East Tennessee State University, through certification with The Institute for Functional Medicine in 2015, and onto the board of the American Academy of Environmental Medicine, where he has served since 2017.

Since 2011 he has served the autism community through The Johnson Center in Austin, Texas — working with families where conventional approaches had run out of answers, and where careful measurement and root-cause repair still had plenty to offer.

I aim to be the physician I would choose for myself. — Dane Mosher, DO
