Dane Mosher · DO

The physician

At fifteen, Dane Mosher read Life Extension by Durk Pearson and Sandy Shaw, and a door opened that never closed: health is not a mystery to be endured. It can be measured, understood, and gently engineered.

That conviction carried him through Family Medicine residency at East Tennessee State University, certification with the Institute for Functional Medicine, and onto the board of the American Academy of Environmental Medicine. Since 2011 he has walked with the autism community through The Johnson Center in Austin, Texas — where root-cause medicine still had answers after everything else went quiet.
